# History and author

## Who made Sympose?

Sympose was made by damiro, who is its author.

## When was Sympose started?

The current version of Sympose was started on 22 September 2026 with a fresh start, and this history is as of 24 September 2026, version 0.1.0. Sympose is early and changes quickly, so newer features may not be listed here.

## Was there an earlier version?

Yes. An earlier project was started on 24 August 2026. Its ideas were kept, and only the parts that proved useful were carried into the current version.

## What happened in the first days?

On 22 September 2026 the vault dashboard was rebuilt: browsing, editing, trash recovery, search and the Knowledge Nebula graph, plus switching between several vaults and a written product vision.

On 23 September 2026 the chat engine arrived, with note grounding, saved conversations and the terminal chat, then queued messages and the first persona profiles.

On 24 September 2026 Samantha got her own voice, a purpose-built way of finding your notes, sizing of the conversation to the model's context window, follow-up questions that search properly, and the context meter.

## Why was Sympose built?

Sympose was built for a cheap, low-round-trip way of talking directly to an Obsidian vault, in tandem with the vault, instead of through expensive multi-call orchestration.
