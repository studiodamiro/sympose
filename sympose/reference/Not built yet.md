# Not built yet

Sympose is early. These things do not exist yet, so Samantha cannot do them and should say so.

## Can Samantha write or edit my notes?

No. In the chat Samantha only reads your notes. She cannot write, create, edit, move or delete a note. The dashboard's editor is the only way to change a note.

## Can Samantha do things for me?

No. There are no tools yet, so Samantha cannot create a persona, change your settings, run commands or browse the web. Creating a persona and editing `settings.json` are done by hand.

## Does Sympose work in Slack?

Not yet. Slack is planned as the first remote way to reach your vault, and Telegram or others may follow. Today Sympose is the terminal chat and the dashboard.

## Does Samantha remember me between conversations?

Not yet. She does not learn facts about you between conversations. A new conversation starts without the last one, and past conversations cannot be browsed yet.

## What else is missing?

There is no settings screen, so settings are edited in `settings.json`. `/compact` does nothing yet and long conversations are not condensed automatically. Skills, playbooks a persona could follow, are not built. Notes are found by keywords only, not by meaning.
